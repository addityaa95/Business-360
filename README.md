## Business 360 Dashboard :

-**Project Overview :**

-**This project analyzes the end-to-end business performance of AtliQ Hardware, a global FMCG company manufacturing electronic products such as computers, mobiles, and accessories. The management was relying on Excel spreadsheets, which made it difficult to monitor performance and make strategic decisions.**

-**To solve this, a comprehensive multi-view interactive Power BI dashboard was developed by integrating data from Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive departments. This dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify performance gaps, and make data-driven decisions efficiently.**

-**The project demonstrates the complete data analytics lifecycle including data collection, cleaning, modeling, DAX calculations, visualization, and stakeholder-focused dashboard development.**

## Project Objectives :

 - [x] Transform static Excel reports into an interactive Power BI dashboard
- [x] Provide centralized visibility across Finance, Sales, Marketing, and Supply Chain
- [x] Monitor key KPIs such as Net Sales, Gross Margin %, Net Profit %, and Forecast Accuracy
- [x] Identify top-performing customers, products, and markets
- [x] Analyze profitability, operational efficiency, and supply chain performance
- [x] Enable executives to make faster and data-driven strategic decisions

## Dashboard Views & Key Insights

-**Executive View Insights :**

 - [x] Net Sales reached 267.98M, but Net Profit % declined to -0.85%, indicating rising operational and production costs impacting profitability
- [x] Top customers like Amazon and AtliQ Exclusive contribute significantly to revenue, highlighting dependency on key enterprise clients

-**Finance View Insights :**

 - [x] Gross Margin remained at 37.10%, but Net Profit declined due to increased operational expenses and cost structure inefficiencies
- [x] APAC region generated the highest revenue contribution, showing strong regional business performance

-**Sales View Insights :**

 - [x] Products like AQ Wi Power Dx2 and AQ BZ Gen Y are top revenue contributors, making them key drivers of company sales
- [x] Sales performance varies across customers and regions, highlighting opportunities to optimize customer targeting strategies

-**Marketing View Insights :**

 - [x] Markets like Philippines, Australia, and France delivered strong profit margins, indicating effective regional marketing strategies
- [x] Some regions show declining Net Profit %, indicating the need for improved pricing and promotional strategies

-**Supply Chain View Insights :**

 - [x] Forecast Accuracy dropped to 72.99%, indicating demand forecasting inefficiencies impacting inventory planning
- [x] Certain product segments show high forecast errors, increasing operational risks and supply chain inefficiencies
